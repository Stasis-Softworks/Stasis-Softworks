## About Stasis

**Stasis Softworks** is an independent software development organisation dedicated to designing, developing, and maintaining software.

We focus on turning ideas into functional, reliable, and thoughtfully engineered digital solutions.

From experimental projects to practical applications, our goal is simple: build software that serves a purpose.

## What We Do

- **Software Engineering** — Developing reliable and maintainable software.
- **Application Development** — Creating practical tools and applications.
- **Open Source** — Sharing projects and contributing to the developer community.
- **Research & Development** — Exploring technologies, frameworks, and new possibilities.
- **Automation & Tooling** — Building utilities that simplify workflows and solve problems.

## Our Approach

We believe good software is built on strong foundations.

Our development philosophy revolves around:

- Clean, readable, and maintainable code.
- Functionality over unnecessary complexity.
- Continuous learning and improvement.
- Thoughtful design and engineering.
- Building solutions with long-term potential.
